# Industrial Machine Compatibility & System Integration Guide
**Landfill Separation Data System • 6-Chute Automated Materials Recovery**

---

## 1. Executive Overview

The **Landfill Separation Data System** is a unified industrial dataset collection, multi-modal item registration, and sensor-fusion simulation platform. It bridges the gap between raw waste collection and the physical deployment of automated sorting machinery in **Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs)**, **Solid Waste Management Plants**, and **High-Speed Recycling Lines**.

This document specifies the mechanical, optical, and algorithmic requirements for deploying datasets and models generated by this system onto physical sorting machinery.

---

## 2. Applicable Machinery Classifications

This platform produces computer vision manifests (YOLOv8, COCO), physical dimensions (height, width, depth, weight), and sensor-fusion signatures (inductive, NIR wavelength, dielectric, hardness) engineered for four primary classes of industrial sorting equipment:

```
[ Infeed Conveyor / Chute (2.0 - 4.5 m/s) ]
                    │
   ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
   ▼                                 ▼
High-Speed Optical             AI Robotic Delta
Pneumatic Air-Jet Sorters      Pick-and-Place Cells
(TOMRA, STEINERT, Pellenc)     (AMP Robotics, ZenRobotics)
   │                                 │
   ▼                                 ▼
Multi-Sensor Fusion Separators Edge Quality Auditing Cameras
(Eddy Current + NIR Spectrometer) (NVIDIA Jetson / Hailo / SICK)
```

### Type A: High-Speed Optical Chute Sorters
* **Operating Principle**: Fast-moving gravity chutes or conveyor belts (operating at 2.0 to 4.5 m/s) accelerate materials beneath high-resolution line-scan RGB cameras and NIR spectrometers. Once classified, an array of high-speed solenoid pneumatic air-jet nozzles (typically 32 to 128 valves spaced 12.5mm to 25mm apart) fires high-pressure compressed air (6–8 bar) to eject target objects into their assigned chute.
* **Compatibility with this Program**:
  - Direct mapping to the **6-Chute Routing Matrix**:
    - **Chute #1**: Plastics (PET, HDPE, PP, LDPE)
    - **Chute #2**: Metals (Iron, Steel, Aluminium)
    - **Chute #3**: Glass (Flint, Amber, Emerald cullet)
    - **Chute #4**: Wood & Fibers (Paper, Cardboard, Timber)
    - **Chute #5**: Textiles (Synthetic & Natural fibers)
    - **Chute #6**: General Waste & Non-Sortable Residue (Negative run-off)
  - **Racur Imagery**: The 5 registered camera racurs (top, front, side, isometric, base) match the tumbling trajectory of items falling through the optical scanning window.
* **Compatible Commercial Machinery**:
  - **TOMRA Sorting**: *AUTOSORT™ (NIR + VIS)*, *FINDER™*
  - **STEINERT**: *UniSort PR EVO 5.0*, *KSS Multi-Sensor Sorter*
  - **Pellenc ST**: *Mistral+ Film & Rigid Polyolefin Sorter*
  - **REDWAVE**: *REDWAVE 2i NIR/Color Chute & Belt Sorter*

### Type B: Vision-Guided AI Robotic Sorting Cells
* **Operating Principle**: High-speed industrial delta robots, SCARA robots, or articulated 6-axis arms mounted over sorting conveyors. Overhead stereoscopic or 2D/3D cameras identify target items, calculate grasp coordinates and surface normals, and trigger vacuum grippers or mechanical claws to pick objects at rates of 40–80 picks per minute.
* **Compatibility with this Program**:
  - **Object Dimensions & Bounding Volumes**: Real-time height ($H$), width ($W$), depth ($D$), and estimated mass metrics inform robot trajectory planning and payload limits.
  - **Multi-Angle & 360° AI Rotation**: Provides multi-view training data essential for training 6-DoF pose estimation networks and calculating optimal pick surface normals.
  - **COCO & YOLOv8 Annotation Formats**: Directly ingestible by standard deep neural network training pipelines (e.g., PyTorch, TensorRT).
* **Compatible Commercial Machinery**:
  - **AMP Robotics**: *AMP Cortex™ Dual-Gantry Sorting System*
  - **ZenRobotics**: *ZenRobotics Fast Picker & Heavy Picker (ZRR2)*
  - **EverestLabs**: *IBrain™ AI Robot Sorting Cell*
  - **Machinex**: *SamurAI™ Sorting Robot*
  - **Bollegraaf Recycling Solutions**: *RoBB Automated Sorting System*

---

### Type C: Multi-Sensor Sensor-Fusion Separators
* **Operating Principle**: Machines combining optical line-scan cameras with physical, electromagnetic, and acoustic sensors to separate complex composite materials that cannot be distinguished by color alone (e.g., distinguishing aluminium cans from tin-plated steel, or transparent PVC from clear PET).
* **Compatibility with this Program's Sensor Telemetry Signatures**:
  - **Inductive Coils (0–100 mV)**: Calibrates Eddy current separators and inductive sensor bars beneath the conveyor belt to differentiate non-ferrous conductors (Aluminium ~85 mV) from non-conductive plastics (0 mV).
  - **Near-Infrared (NIR / SWIR) Absorption Peaks**: Specific polymer spectral signatures (e.g., 1660 nm for PET, 1730 nm for PP, 1450 nm for Cellulose paper) calibrate optical filter wheels and hyperspectral sensor banks.
  - **Dielectric & Permittivity Values**: Differentiates moisture-laden textiles and paper from dry rigid plastics.
  - **Impact / Tap Acoustic Hardness (0–100)**: Used in mechanical ballistic separators and acoustic plate sorters (e.g., rigid glass shards ~90 vs. soft plastic films ~15).
* **Compatible Commercial Machinery**:
  - **MSS, Inc. (CP Group)**: *CIRRUS® Multi-Sensor Optical Sorter*
  - **Binder+Co**: *CLARITY & MINEX Sensor-Fusion Sorters*
  - **Eriez**: *Eddy Current Separator with Integrated Induction Sensor Bar*

---

### Type D: Edge Industrial Quality Control & Stream Auditing Stations
* **Operating Principle**: Fixed overhead camera enclosures installed on transfer conveyors and quality control points to monitor purity rates, detect toxic/hazardous objects (e.g., lithium batteries, propane canisters), and audit landfill chute contamination.
* **Compatibility with this Program**:
  - Exported YOLOv8 models compile directly to edge runtimes including **NVIDIA TensorRT** on Jetson Orin / Xavier, **Intel OpenVINO**, and **Hailo-8** edge AI accelerators.
  - Standardized image dimensions and racur tags ensure consistent classification across changing facility lighting conditions.
* **Compatible Hardware Platforms**:
  - **Industrial Cameras**: *Basler ace / dart series*, *Cognex In-Sight 2800/3800*, *SICK inspectorP6xx*, *FLIR Blackfly S (GigE/USB3)*.
  - **Edge Processors**: *NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin Industrial*, *Advantech MIC-770 V3*, *Siemens SIMATIC IPC520A*.

## 4. Hardware Integration & PLC Interface Protocols

Datasets and models trained with this system communicate with industrial automation systems through standardized protocols:

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  AI Inference Engine         │  YOLOv8 / TensorRT Pipeline
│  (NVIDIA Jetson / Edge IPC)  │  Object Center (X, Y) + Chute ID
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │ Industrial Ethernet (1 Gbps)
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  Programmable Logic Control  │  Siemens S7-1500 / Allen-Bradley ControlLogix
│  (PLC Real-Time Controller)  │  Belt Encoder Speed Sync (1 ms cycle)
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │ Fast Digital I/O (24 V DC)
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  Actuator Manifold           │  High-Speed Solenoid Pneumatic Valves
│  (6-Chute Sorting System)    │  Response Time < 5 ms
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Supported Industrial Protocols:
1. **OPC UA (IEC 62541)**: Publishes detected object attributes, chute destination IDs, classification confidence (0.00–1.00), and timestamped coordinates.
2. **Modbus TCP / PROFINET / Ethernet/IP**: Direct cyclic data exchange with Siemens S7-1200/1500, Allen-Bradley ControlLogix, and Beckhoff TwinCAT PLCs.
3. **ROS2 (Robot Operating System)**: Publishes `/detected_waste_items` messages with 3D bounding boxes and center-of-mass coordinates for robotic arm path planning.
4. **MQTT / Sparkplug B**: Telemetry logging for SCADA systems, facility dashboards, and daily recovery throughput analytics.

## 5. Vision Racur Calibration Guidelines

When capturing or validating training samples for physical sorting machines:

| Racur Angle | Camera Position | Optical Purpose in Sorting Machine |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top (90°)** | Perpendicular to conveyor belt | Primary detection angle for planar line-scan and overhead area-scan cameras. |
| **Isometric (45°)** | Oblique angle facing belt travel | Simulates optical sorter perspective when objects are at the camera FOV perimeter. |
| **Front (0°)** | Parallel to chute surface | Critical for detecting flat, compressed, or rolled packaging (e.g., flattened cans). |
| **Side (90°)** | Perpendicular lateral view | Distinguishes container depth and prevents misclassifying flat paper as 3D boxes. |
| **Base / Bottom** | Reverse profile | Identifies recycling resin identification codes (RIC 1–7) stamped into container bases. |

## 6. How to Export Datasets for Machine Training

1. Navigate to **Export & Sync** in the application navigation header.
2. Select your target architecture:
   - **YOLOv8 Bundle**: Generates `data.yaml` and normalized bounding box text files suitable for Ultralytics YOLOv8/v9/v11.
   - **COCO Manifest**: Generates standard JSON annotations for Detectron2, MMDetection, and TensorFlow Object Detection API.
   - **Combined CSV / JSON**: Raw multi-modal sensor signatures for tabular classifiers (XGBoost, Random Forest) used in sensor-fusion sorting.
3. Click **Export All Datasets** or copy the configuration directly to your training workstation.
